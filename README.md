🌌 AEGIS SKY — SEE MORE. DETECT MORE.
🚀 Discover a new way to explore the sky!
Aegis Sky is an advanced Android sky-surveillance and multi-spectral observation application designed to enhance your sky-watching experience with powerful optical tools, real-time computer vision, and intelligent tracking technology.
🔭 EXPLORE ITS FEATURES:
🟢 Night Vision — Enhanced green phosphor mode (NVG-GRN).
🔴 Red Spectrum — Red night observation mode.
🌡️ Infrared — IR-850 false-color visualization.
⚪ Black & White — High-contrast monochrome imaging.
🎯 Real-Time Detection — Identify and track luminous points and moving objects.
📡 Kinematic Tracking — Motion analysis, trajectories, and Kalman filtering.
📸 4K Photography & Video — Capture and record processed imagery.
🔌 External USB-OTG Camera — Expand your optical capabilities with compatible external cameras.
🛰️ GPS & Astrometric Telemetry — Azimuth, elevation, RA and DEC information.
🔐 Evidence Vault — Store and organize your captured observations.
Built for Android. Powered by Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and OpenGL ES 2.0.
